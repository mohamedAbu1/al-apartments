## AI video prompt

Create a 60-second premium cinematic tourism film for Egypt and the Al-Aroum travel brand. Show the Giza pyramids at sunrise, the Sphinx, a Nile felucca, Luxor temples, ancient hieroglyphics, Cairo streets, Egyptian markets, Red Sea coral reefs, turquoise beaches, desert dunes, Siwa oasis, and a Mediterranean sunset. Blend history, adventure, culture, food, warmth, and luxury travel. Use slow gimbal shots, elegant drone transitions, shallow depth of field, natural human movement, soft film grain, warm gold, sand, turquoise, and deep navy color grading. The mood is cinematic, sophisticated, emotional, and welcoming. No distorted faces, no unreadable AI text, no floating architecture, no inaccurate monuments, no exaggerated clichés. 16:9, 4K, premium tourism advertising.

## Voice direction

Male English voice, 35–45 years old, warm cinematic baritone, calm confidence, articulate international pronunciation, intimate studio recording, subtle wonder, no aggressive sales tone. Pace: approximately 125–135 words per minute. Add light room warmth and very subtle compression.

## Music and sound

Begin with soft piano and airy ambient pads. Add gentle organic percussion at 0:25, a restrained cinematic swell at 0:45, and resolve with one warm piano note at the logo reveal. Add subtle sea ambience, distant wind, and soft camera movement sound design. Keep music below the voice at all times.
